ResumeRise

A truthfulness-constrained coach for resume–job matching and interview practice

Candidate-side coach. Advice only, not hiring decisions. Remove contact details before pasting. Nothing is stored by this app; text is sent only to the LLM endpoint you configure.

How it works

  • Keyword baseline – the top 20 non-stop-word terms of the job description by frequency; coverage is the share found in your resume. Runs fully offline.
  • LLM analysis – structured JSON: score (skills 40, experience 30, education 15, communication evidence 15), strengths, must-have / nice-to-have gaps with honest actions, three bullet rewrites and clarity flags.
  • Truthfulness – the model must use only facts in your resume. Each rewrite quotes the original line, and the app checks that the quote exists in your resume and flags numbers that do not.
  • Mock interview – 5 questions (2 behavioural, 2 technical, 1 project). Each answer is scored 1–5 on relevance, structure, specificity and communication; a session average is shown. Length is not rewarded.
  • Robustness – resume, job and answers are treated as data; embedded instructions are ignored. All model output is rendered as plain text.

Scores estimate similarity to a posting, not hiring outcomes. Verify every statement on your resume.

LLM settings

Any OpenAI-compatible /chat/completions endpoint works. Pick a preset or use a custom URL. For fully local use, run Ollama.
